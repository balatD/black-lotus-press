Your Moxfield deck, printed.

Black Lotus Press turns Moxfield decks into print-ready playtest cards and hands them to the print shop you choose: meinspiel.de, notmpc.eu or printerstudio.de. It takes the exact printings from Scryfall or high-resolution images from MPC Autofill, cleans them up, upscales them and makes them the way each shop wants them.

macOS Apple Silicon Windows 64-bit Linux AppImage

Free and open source under the MIT license. Portable ZIPs, the command-line tool and release notes are on the release page.

On a Mac: the app is not signed with an Apple certificate, so macOS says it "is damaged" or that Apple "could not verify" it. It is neither. Drag it into Applications, then run this once in Terminal:

xattr -cr "/Applications/Black Lotus Press.app"

From deck list to press sheet

Everything happens on your computer. The app only goes online to load decks, card images and card backs, to install Upscayl and to upload, and only when you ask it to.

  1. Import your decks

    Paste a Moxfield link and pick the boards you want: main board, sideboard, tokens, attractions and the rest. The app takes the exact printings from the deck and looks them up on Scryfall.

    • Several decks in one order, each imported on its own.
    • Foils from Moxfield can be swapped for non-foil printings right after the import.
    • Update a deck later without losing your edits.

  2. Check every card

    See the whole order as a list or as a zoomable grid. Click a card to open the card editor: every printing on Scryfall with Cardmarket prices, the high-resolution images of MPC Autofill, or your own image, large and side by side with the Scryfall scan.

    • Zoom both images at once, down to the pixel.
    • MPC Autofill images sorted by whether they show the card's printing, with their resolution.
    • The sharpest MPC Autofill image for every card in one go.
    • Different printings for single copies, quantities, and the print order by drag and drop.
    • Warnings for low-resolution scans and cards Scryfall does not know.

  3. Proof the press sheet

    Every pack or deck appears as a sheet, fronts and backs, the way it will be printed: 55 cards for meinspiel.de, the deck size for notmpc.eu and printerstudio.de. Brightness and contrast change the preview right away, for the whole order or for single cards.

    • Upscaling with live GPU load and temperature.
    • Process a single card or the whole order again.
    • For meinspiel.de, two PDFs per pack, with their sizes checked against the upload limit.

  4. Hand it to the shop

    The shop opens in a browser window that looks like part of the app, with a panel that shows every step and how far the upload is, and the app does the uploading. You choose the options and pay there yourself; the app never orders anything.

    • meinspiel.de: every pack becomes a game in your account. You log in yourself, the app never sees your password.
    • printerstudio.de: you log in, and their designer is filled up to the preview and saved in your account, one deck each; you confirm the copyright and put the decks into the cart.
    • notmpc.eu: their designer opens with every card in its slot, no account needed; not available at the moment.
    • Or save the order as an MPC Autofill XML for its desktop tool, which orders at MakePlayingCards.

The details that make the prints good

  • Clean scansRounded corners are filled, scan borders cropped and holo stamps painted over. Borderless art is mirrored into the bleed instead of smeared.
  • Sharper with MPC AutofillImages from MPC Autofill, usually at 800 to 1200 dpi, recognised against the Scryfall scans of every printing so custom art stays apart.
  • Your copyright lineKeep the original, blur it, add "Proxy – not for sale", or write your own text in its place.
  • Any card backAll card backs of MPC Autofill in one gallery, searchable by name or creator, with their resolution. Double-faced cards get their real back.
  • Tokens as fillerTokens fill the free slots of the last pack or deck and never cost you an extra pack or a larger deck.
  • The format of each shopCMYK PDFs for meinspiel.de, sRGB for notmpc.eu, and MPC's 2.72 × 3.70 inches for printerstudio.de, the size its designer asks for.
  • Seven upscaling modelsUpscayl installs with one click. Pick the model that suits your cards; the lite model is seven times faster.
  • Runs in the backgroundImports, processing and uploads run as tasks you can follow and cancel, while you keep editing.

Questions

Is Black Lotus Press affiliated with meinspiel.de, notmpc.eu, printerstudio.de, Moxfield, Scryfall or Wizards of the Coast?

No. It is a free fan project. It uses their public websites the way you would in a browser.

Which print shop should I choose?

meinspiel.de suits decks of 55 or 110 cards and prints from CMYK PDFs. printerstudio.de prints decks of any size up to 612 cards, so a Commander deck is one deck instead of two packs. You need an account at both. notmpc.eu, which needs none, is not available at the moment. The Cards tab shows what an order costs at its shop.

What does printing cost?

A 100-card Commander deck costs about 39,90 € at meinspiel.de as two packs of 55, about 29,97 € at notmpc.eu as one deck of 108, and about 31,30 € at printerstudio.de as one deck of 108 on Standard stock, plus shipping. Check the shops for the current prices.

Can I sell the cards I print?

No. The card images belong to Wizards of the Coast and the artists. Print them for your own playtesting only.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. Without upscaling, any computer works. Upscaling at 600 dpi uses the graphics card; on an Apple M1 Max it takes 2 to 14 seconds per card, depending on the model.
